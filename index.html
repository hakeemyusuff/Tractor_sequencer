How priority is calculated
Daily loss (₦/day)
Area × yield × price × daily loss fraction. Divide the percentage above by 100 to get the fraction.
Job time (hours)
Area ÷ field capacity, plus travel and setup.
Priority score
Daily loss ÷ job time. Higher scores go first; tied farms keep booking order.
Estimated loss for a schedule
For each farm, multiply its daily loss by its finish day. Add those losses for all farms.

With a delay limit, each farm must finish within the chosen number of working days of its booking-order finish. The planner chooses the highest-priority next job that leaves all deadlines achievable. It may retain booking order if that gives a lower loss.

Assumptions and sources

One tractor ploughs fields that are all ready at the start. Planting is due immediately and is assumed to wait until ploughing finishes. Delay costs are linear. A finish day is elapsed tractor hours divided by working hours per day.

Travel and setup use the same allowance for each farm, including the first. The schedule does not model routes, weather or breakdowns.

The example farms are synthetic. Default loss rates for rice, maize and soybean are historical US seeding examples; the cowpea rate, all prices and all yields are assumptions.
